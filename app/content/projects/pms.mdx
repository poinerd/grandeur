<Section label="01 · The problem" title="No one could see the whole picture">

TM30 was running 20+ projects at once, with progress scattered across spreadsheets and files. Leadership had no single place to answer: how are our projects doing?

</Section>

<Section label="02 · The solution" title="One tool, built for three kinds of people">

TM30 PMS replaces the spreadsheets with one place to plan projects, assign work and see where everything stands. I designed every screen in Figma, built part of the interface in React and contributed to the Django backend.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card eyebrow="Executives" title="See everything">

  </Card>
  <Card eyebrow="Project managers" title="Manage the work">

</Section>

TM30 PMS is now how TM30 tracks its projects, and new screens and features are still being added. Working directly with executives, managers and developers taught me that the best design decisions come from listening early and iterating fast.
