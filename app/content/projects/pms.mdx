## Overview

TM30 PMS is a project management tool built in-house for PMS. It gives the team one place to plan projects, assign work and see where everything stands, replacing scattered spreadsheets, chats and status calls.

## The problem

Project information was spread across different tools and conversations. Nobody had a single reliable view of what was in progress, who owned what, or what was late. Updating status meant asking people, and decisions were made on information that was already out of date.

## The solution

TM30 PMS brings the project workflow into one internal product:

- Create projects and break them into tasks.
- Assign owners and track status as work moves forward.
- See progress across projects at a glance.

Because it was built specifically for PMS, it follows the way the team already works instead of forcing them into a generic tool's structure.

<Callout>
  Built in-house, so every screen and workflow is shaped around how PMS actually runs projects.
</Callout>

## Design process

I started by understanding how the team tracked work before the tool existed, then mapped the main flows: creating a project, assigning tasks, and checking progress. From there I designed the key screens and refined them with feedback from the people who would use it every day.

## Key decisions

**Built around the team's workflow.** Using an in-house tool meant I could remove everything the team didn't need and keep the screens focused on daily use.

**Clarity over features.** Status, owner and deadline are always visible, so anyone can understand a project's state without asking.

**One source of truth.** All project updates live in the same place, which removes the confusion of conflicting versions.

## What I learned

Designing for an internal team showed me how much a tool's value comes from fitting real habits, and how direct access to users makes feedback faster and the product better.
