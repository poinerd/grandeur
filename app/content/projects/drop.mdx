<Section label="01 · The problem" title="Moving files shouldn't be this complicated">

Getting a design export from a computer onto a phone usually means uploading it somewhere, opening another app, or sending it through a chat, and then doing it all again for the next file.

</Section>

<Section label="02 · The solution" title="A folder becomes a file portal">

Hop runs a lightweight Go server on your computer. Scan a QR code on your phone and a browser-based interface opens, where you can browse, preview and download files, or upload them back. No app, no account, no cloud.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card eyebrow="01 · Connect" title="Scan the QR code">
    Link your phone to the local server.
  </Card>
  <Card eyebrow="02 · Browse" title="Find a file">
    Explore files in your mobile browser.
  </Card>
  <Card eyebrow="03 · Transfer" title="Move it across">
    Download to your phone or upload to your computer.
  </Card>
</Columns>

</Section>
